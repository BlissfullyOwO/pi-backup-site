# Privacy Policy — Raspberry Pi Backup

**Last updated: October 9, 2026**

## Overview

Raspberry Pi Backup is a personal, non-commercial application used to back up files and system configurations from a privately operated Raspberry Pi 5 to the owner's Google Drive account.

## Google Drive Access

The application uses Rclone to authenticate with Google Drive and transfer backup data.

Google Drive permissions are used to create, access, maintain, and restore backups. Depending on the permissions granted during authorization, Rclone may have access to other files in the authorized Google Drive account.

## Data Storage and Security

Backup data is encrypted using Restic before being uploaded to Google Drive. Authentication credentials and encryption keys are stored locally on the Raspberry Pi.

Google Drive is used as the storage provider. The application does not sell personal information or share it with advertisers.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Backups are retained according to the owner's configured retention policy. The owner can delete stored backups and revoke Google Drive access through their Google account settings.

## Usage

This application is intended solely for personal use and is not offered as a public service.

## Policy Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data-handling practices change.
